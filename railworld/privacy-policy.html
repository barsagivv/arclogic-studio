Privacy Policy

RailWorld · ArcLogic Studio · Last updated 10 September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ArcLogic Studio ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the mobile game RailWorld (the "App"). By downloading or using the App you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

The App does not require you to create an account, and we do not intentionally collect personal information that directly identifies you. There is no login, no profile and no chat. Your game progress is stored locally on your device.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to show two kinds of advertising: rewarded videos, which you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward, and a banner displayed at the bottom of the screen. AdMob and Google Play services may collect and process certain data automatically, including:

  • Advertising identifier (Advertising ID) and device identifiers
  • Approximate, non-precise information used to serve and measure ads
  • Diagnostics such as crash logs and performance data

Until you have been asked for consent where the law requires it, ads are requested as non-personalised.

Analytics

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played in aggregate — for example which parts of the game people reach and where they stop. This records app activity together with device and app identifiers. It is not linked to a real-world identity, and we do not attempt to identify individual players from it.

Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases. All payments are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card, billing address or any other payment details — we are only told that a purchase completed, so the App can deliver what was bought.

Notifications

With your permission, the App may send notifications about your own game — for example that your line has reached its offline earnings cap. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

How the information is used

Collected data is used to display advertising, to measure ad performance, to prevent fraud, to understand how the game is played, and to keep the App working reliably. We do not sell your data.

Third-party services

Advertising, analytics and payment services are provided by Google. Their handling of data is governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use our services
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

Children

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads). Buying the App's "No ads" product removes both the banner and the rewarded videos.

Data retention & deletion

Locally stored game progress remains on your device and can be removed by uninstalling the App. For requests related to advertising or analytics data, please contact us at the email below and we will act on them within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.
