Lure Line

Fishing Puzzle · by DoublePixel

A calm daily logic puzzle about fishing. Draw one line from your boat through every water tile, hook the fish from smallest to biggest, and finish on the trophy. Then reel in and see what you caught.

How it plays

  • Every puzzle has exactly one solution and never needs a guess.
  • The daily pond is the same for every player and changes at your local midnight. Keep your streak going.
  • Your catches fill a fish log and your own aquarium.
  • Weekly leagues, the weekend derby (Friday to Sunday) and world leaderboards. Players appear with a nickname made from fixed words and a drawn avatar. There is no chat.
  • Relaxed mode hides clocks and times, and colour-blind symbols are available.
  • Plays offline; the online features work when you are connected.
  • In English, Hebrew, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Japanese and Korean.

Free to play

Lure Line is free and supported by ads: reward videos you choose to watch, and full-screen ads between levels after the first levels. Optional purchases: Remove Ads, pearls, the Starter Pack, streak freezes and the Angler Club subscription. For Android phones (Google Play) and iPhone (App Store).
