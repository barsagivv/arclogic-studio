The App does not require you to create an account and we do not intentionally collect personal information that directly identifies you. Game progress is stored locally on your device.

Collected data is used to display advertising (including personalized ads where permitted), to measure ad performance, to prevent fraud, and to keep the App working reliably.

Third-party services

Advertising and core services are provided by Google. Their handling of data is governed by Google's own policies:

Children

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

Data retention & deletion

Locally stored game progress remains on your device and can be removed by uninstalling the App. For requests related to advertising data, please contact us at the email below.
